## Issues Found

### 1. Sonos Cron Failure (Medium)
**Status**: Open  
**Location**: `trmnl-sonos-local` cron job on khpi5 (runs every minute)  
**Symptom**: `RuntimeError: No Sonos speakers discovered`  
**Root cause**: `soco.discover()` fails to find speakers on network, but Home Assistant has 5 Sonos entities registered:
- `media_player.living_room`
- `media_player.kitchen`
- `media_player.gym`
- `media_player.bedroom`
- `media_player.sonos_roam`

**Architecture conflict**:
- HA automation `trmnl_sonos_push_from_ha_v1` is **disabled** (alias: "Disabled for Local Plugin Ownership")
- Cron-based approach chosen instead, but failing
- Likely cause: soco discovery blocked by network segmentation or firewall

**Recommendation**: Enable HA automation (change condition from `off` to `on`) and disable cron. HA already has working Sonos integration.

### 2. Nginx Upstream Timeouts (Low)
**Status**: Cosmetic  
**Location**: `/etc/nginx/site-opts.d/http.conf` in LaraPaper container  
**Symptom**: Healthcheck endpoint occasionally times out (2 occurrences today: 00:33, 05:49)  
**Impact**: Container shows healthy despite slow healthcheck responses. No impact on real traffic.
